# Maple Cream Pie

## Ingredients - 2 pies

- 6	eggs
- 6	tablespoons flour
- 3	cups maple syrup
- 1-½	cups heavy cream
- 2	9-inch pie shells, partially baked
- whipped cream for topping

## Ingredients - 1 pie

- 3	eggs
- 3	tablespoons flour
- 1-½	cups maple syrup
- 3/4	cups heavy cream
- 1	9-inch pie shells, partially baked
- whipped cream for topping


## Directions:

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. 

2. (PARTIALLY BAKE THE CRUST)

2. Beat the eggs, adding flour gradually. 
   While beating, slowly pour in the syrup and cream. 
   Pour this into the pie shells. 
   
3. Bake pies 40 minutes. 
   Cool in fridge until at least room temperature. 
   Cover the whole pie in 2-3" of whipped cream (minimum).
   
You can eat right away. but it is better if the whole pie sits overnight in the fridge. 

Optimal time to eat is at breakfast with coffee. 

## Partially Baked Crust

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. 

2. Prepare the shell for baking. 

   - Poke the bottom of the crust with a fork to avoid bubbling. 
   - Place tinfoil in the bottom and add pie weights, beans, or rice. 
   - Place tin foil around the edges to avoid burning the crust. 

3. Bake for 20-25 minutes. 

   Remove the pie weights.
